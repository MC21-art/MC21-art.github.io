In 2020, Lipina has proven that, given a deterministic, letter-to-letter, functional transducer on an alphabet of size 2, such that its generated function preserves the prefixes, 
the problem of whether its generated function is ergodic is decidable. We extend this result, by weakening the hypothesis,to non-deterministic and non letter-to-letter transducer, 
and show that the resulting algorithm has an exponential complexity. We futhermore show how to optimize parts the algorithm that had an exponential complexity, to reach, for these parts,
a polynomial complexity.

This is a joint work with Olivier Carton and Matthieu Picantin.
